Projects & research

A Scalable Geometric Deep Learning Approach to Weather Forecasting

2026

MSc thesis, University of Amsterdam. Supervisor David Wessels, examiner Erik Bekkers. Grade 8.5.

How geometric inductive biases shape weather-model skill and physical fidelity, isolating one design choice at a time on global ERA5. Introduces Platonic-Erwin, a linearly-scaling ball-tree transformer with roto-translation group equivariance. Symmetry helps as an inductive bias but not as a hard constraint: scale buys deterministic skill, geometry buys spectral fidelity, calibration and parameter efficiency.

Thesis PDF Code

Reproducing NevIR: Negation in Neural Information Retrieval

2025

Published at SIGIR 2025, University of Amsterdam.

Replicated and extended NevIR to assess how IR models handle negation. Only cross-encoders and listwise LLM re-rankers reached moderate performance, still below human level, and fine-tuning across negation datasets generalised poorly.

Geometric Algebra Simplicial Transformer

2023

Deep Learning 2 project, University of Amsterdam.

Proposed a novel class of equivariant transformers, using simplicial complexes and Clifford algebra to capture full symmetry group equivariance.
